LinkedIn Text Formatter

v0.1.0 100% Local

How to Use

  1. Open LinkedIn in your browser.
  2. Start writing a post in the composer.
  3. Highlight the text you wish to format.
  4. Click a style on the floating toolbar.

Supported Scope: Version 1 supports LinkedIn's Create a Post editor only. Comments, messaging, and article editors are not currently supported.

Accessibility Note

Styled text uses Unicode characters rather than native bold or italic formatting. Use it mainly for headings and short phrases because some screen readers may not interpret it normally.

🔒

Privacy Guaranteed

Your text is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded, stored, or sent to a server.
